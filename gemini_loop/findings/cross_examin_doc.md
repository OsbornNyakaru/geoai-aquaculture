## Key Findings

1. **Every arXiv/journal citation in both documents was checked. Two of Gemini's are hallucinated-in-context or domain-irrelevant.** CropNet (arXiv:2509.03497), Set Transformer (1810.00825), TimeMatch (Nyborg et al., ISPRS 2022), Match-and-Deform (2308.12686), Deep CORAL (Sun & Saenko), Saerens/Alexandari MLLS, TENT (Wang et al., ICLR 2021), MiniRocket/Hydra (Dempster et al.), Bake-off-redux — all real and say what is claimed. **PE-Field 4D (arXiv:2607.15667) is a real paper but is about video-diffusion transformers (DiTs) — irrelevant to satellite time series. "Label Shift Estimation With Incremental Prior Update" (arXiv:2604.01651, LEIP, Zhang/Batista/Kanhere) is real but, like all label-shift methods, explicitly assumes p(x|y) is fixed — which is false here. arXiv:2604.19217 could not be verified and should be treated as unverified.**

2. **Gemini's foundational premise is mathematically wrong, and this collapses its entire recommendation #5.** After the prevalence pin fixes the predicted-positive count P̂, F1 = 2·TP/(P̂+P) is a monotone function of precision@P̂ within the top-P̂ set — a functional of the ranking alone. AUC is rank-only by definition. Therefore any monotone recalibration — temperature scaling, a constant logit offset, or the full Saerens-EM/MLLS logit shift — is invisible to the leaderboard except through the single scalar P̂ it implies. MLLS is thus exactly subsumed by your existing prevalence pin; its only possible effect is to choose a *worse* operating point than your LB-verified 0.649. You already measured this failure mode: BBSE picked 0.44.

3. **The champion architecture and the ledger are your ground truth; nothing below ~0.02 LB is measurable at this budget.** The finite-sample sampling SD of 0.6·F1+0.4·AUC on ~309 public rows is ≈0.013–0.018, comparable to your measured seed SD of 0.019. Two candidates within ~0.02 are statistically indistinguishable on the public LB.

### C. CHALLENGE TO CLAUDE R09 (where I was wrong or overconfident)

1. **The variance-collapse / order-statistics argument conflated two distinct variance components.** The 0.019 figure is σ_seed (training stochasticity). The finite-sample *public sampling* noise σ_public — from scoring a fixed prediction vector on ~309 rows — is a SEPARATE, additive component. Using the Hanley–McNeil (1982) SE formula with ~170 positives / 140 negatives, SE(AUC)≈0.018 at AUC 0.90 falling to ≈0.014 at AUC 0.94; the max-variance bound (Cortes & Mohri, NeurIPS 2004) gives σ_max≈0.023; F1 bootstrap/delta-method SD≈0.020–0.025; the composite 0.6·F1+0.4·AUC lands at **≈0.013–0.018** (dominated by the F1 term). A competitor resubmitting the *same* model gets the *same* public score (no resampling), so max-of-n inflation operates only across genuinely different submissions and selects on √(σ_seed²+σ_public²)≈0.024 jointly. **The directional claim survives** — the top-5 public gaps (0.928–0.945, ~0.017 span) are roughly 1σ_public, so a shakeup is plausible and the correct final-selection policy is still to pick low-variance, not high-public. **But my specific "honest private ~0.915–0.925" was over-precise.** Roelofs, Shankar, Recht, Fridovich-Keil, Hardt, Miller & Schmidt, "A Meta-Analysis of Overfitting in Machine Learning" (NeurIPS 2019, pp. 9175–9185) found "little evidence of substantial overfitting" across 100+ Kaggle competitions, but explicitly note the exception: "Due to the limited size of the public and private test sets, the public scores are only approximately equal to the private scores (even in the absence of any adaptive overfitting), which can lead to substantial rank changes even though all score deviations are small" — which is precisely the sub-sampling-noise regime this plan is in. Confidence: medium.
2. **"Rank-averaging strictly dominates probability-averaging" was overstated.** Under a rank-only metric, magnitude genuinely cannot matter (F1 depends only on top-P̂ membership, AUC only on order) — so rank-averaging is provably *invariant* to per-member monotone reparameterization, which probability-averaging is not. That much is correct and is corroborated by top Kaggle write-ups ("rank averaging completely targets relative order, ignoring absolute probability calibration and outlier confidence"). But invariance is not optimality: rank- and probability-averaging produce *different* orderings, and which scores higher is empirical. "Provably the correct combiner" should read "provably the invariant combiner, empirically preferred." Confidence: high on invariance, medium on dominance.
3. **In-domain SSL on 2,847 rows is a bet, not a sure thing.** Ti-MAE (arXiv:2301.08871) and TimeMAE were demonstrated on much larger corpora; Presto (Tseng et al., NeurIPS 2023 CCAI / arXiv:2304.14065, "Lightweight, Pre-trained Transformers for Remote Sensing Timeseries") was pretrained on 21.5 million pixel-timeseries with 12-month contiguous intervals plus static topographic and coordinate data, and has "1000× fewer trainable parameters than ViT- or ResNet-based models." Masked-value SSL at ~2.8k samples may not move the manifold. The free go/no-go (adversarial AUC on resulting embeddings must drop below the 0.965 Presto floor) is the right gate. On rules: transductive use of the *provided* unlabeled test features is standard-accepted (Zindi rule: "You may use only the datasets provided" — test features ARE provided; no external data, no labels used), but it MUST be documented for the Phase-Two reproducibility review. Confidence: medium.
4. **Dispersion-aware pooling as [mean‖std‖min‖max] DOES grow the head input 4× and is therefore predicted by my own ledger law to fail.** Reconciliation: inject dispersion *capacity-neutrally* — REPLACE mean-pool with a 2-way [mean‖std] at the SAME total head width (halve d per branch), or replace mean with std-only. This is amplitude-preserving (keeps the level) and capacity-matched, so it is NOT the detrend experiment and NOT a capacity increase. This aligns with the aquaculture literature: Ottinger, Clauss & Kuenzer (Remote Sensing 2017, 9(5):440) mapped ponds "with an overall accuracy of 0.83," and the follow-on Asian/India work uses pixel-wise temporal median/percentiles/std explicitly to exclude seasonal water (rice paddies) — i.e., the discriminator is temporal permanence/dispersion, not order. Confidence: medium.

### D. WHAT ACTUALLY GETS +0.04

The reliable-level-to-top-5 gap (0.8865 → ~0.928 public) is ≈+0.04, the size of the GBDT→Transformer jump. But **the honest private target is lower than 0.928**: with σ_public≈0.015 and top-5 gaps ~0.017, the public top is partly fluctuation and will compress on the 721-row private set. A realistic defensible private top-5 is plausibly ~0.90–0.92 (modeled estimate, not a measurement). Ranked candidates for a ≥0.02 structural gain:

1. **Instance-expansion reframing (highest EV).** Treat each (row, observed sub-window) as an independent training example and aggregate at inference. This is the only proposal that is BOTH a data-model change (the category that has historically cleared your noise floor) AND directly matched to the trap (test rows ARE partial windows). It multiplies 1,817 fully-observed rows into many masked sub-window views and forces the model to learn from exactly the observation structure the test exposes. A competitor reportedly reached ~0.914 AUC LB with "made each month a training data point" (reported lead, unverified). Confidence: medium-high this is the mechanism separating top-5 from the middle.
2. **Decorrelated low-capacity member (MiniRocket/Hydra OR CropNet), combined by rank.** Only helps if genuinely decorrelated (target ρ 0.6–0.75). Go/no-go: add only if mean pairwise rank-corr <0.9 and solo ATC-F1 within ~0.02 of champion.
3. **In-domain transductive SSL** — see C3.
4. **Radar-only decorrelated sub-model** exploiting the S1-always-present asymmetry.

Phase-Two EV: top-5 only are reviewed for 35% of the final score on a 0–10 innovation+reproducibility rubric. Once you are in private top-5 range, the marginal value of chasing the last 0.005 of LB is below the marginal value of a clean, novel, reproducible narrative (in-domain SSL + instance reframing + rank ensemble is a strong, coherent story). Allocate accordingly.

### E. FINAL RANKED MOVES

**1. Instance-expansion reframing** — Mechanism: each observed sub-window becomes a training example; aggregate (mean of logits over available windows) at inference. Change: data loader emits K masked sub-window views per row as independent examples with the SAME 24-channel encoder; inference pools per-row. Legal: supplied data only, seeded. Not a repeat: no prior ledger entry expands the *instance* axis (K=2→4 masking was augmentation *views*, not independent training examples). Expected band: +0.01 to +0.04 (model-class-adjacent). Screen: OOF ATC-F1 (0.010–0.013 resolution); adversarial-AUC free. **Deserves a submission.**

**2. Decorrelated low-capacity member + rank blend** — Mechanism: MiniRocket/Hydra (contract: never feed −9999; left-align observed window to t=0; edge-pad to 12 with last observed value; append the 12 missing-indicator channels; apply the test masking distribution to train rows) OR CropNet 1D-CNN. Combine by rank. Expected: +0.00 to +0.02, conditional on ρ<0.9. Screen: pairwise rank-corr + solo ATC-F1 (both free/offline). **Submission only if the decorrelation gate passes.**

**3. In-domain masked-value SSL pretraining** — Mechanism: MAE-style masked-value pretraining on all 2,847 rows (1,817 train + 1,030 unlabeled test), then fine-tune the BCE+λ·Var head. Legal, transductive, documented. Expected: +0.00 to +0.03. Screen: adversarial AUC on embeddings must drop <0.95 (free go/no-go). **Submission only if the adversarial gate passes.**

**4. Capacity-neutral dispersion pooling** — Replace mean-pool with [mean‖std] at matched head width. Expected: within noise (±0.01). Screen: ATC-F1. **No dedicated submission; fold into a variant.**

**5. SAR/EATA LayerNorm-compatible TTA (exploratory)** — Adapt only LN affine params, entropy-min, sample-filtered to avoid collapse, fold-safe/seeded. Expected: unknown, possibly >0.02 or negative. Screen: OOF re-ranking magnitude + ATC-F1 before any submission. **Screen only; submit only if the offline screen shows >0.02 re-ranking gain.**

**REJECT (do not spend submissions):** MLLS/Saerens-EM (rank-invisible + assumption-violated), Zou water tree (−0.075 measured), Deep CORAL (destroys the level signal), duration-normalized positions (−0.0064 measured), ISAB/PMA (capacity-add over an exhausted lane), BBSE/prior estimation (rejected 3× already).

### What would falsify this plan
- If the instance-expansion reframing scores ≤ champion ±0.01 on OOF ATC-F1, the "data-model change is the +0.04 lever" thesis is wrong and priority shifts to the ensemble+SSL portfolio.
- If in-domain SSL fails to push adversarial AUC below 0.95, the "in-domain beats off-domain Presto" thesis is falsified.
- If every decorrelated member lands at ρ>0.9, the ensemble lane is as exhausted as seed-averaging was (0.886, landed at the member mean).
- If the private shakeup does NOT materialize (leader holds ~0.945 on private), the finite-sample-noise thesis is wrong and selecting on low variance cost nothing but was unnecessary.
